# Changelog

Newest first. Times are local (Europe/Prague). Entries before 2026-10-24 10:32 were backfilled
on 2026-10-25 from the development log; their times are approximate (when the change was

## 2026-11-02 22:23 CET — the friend does everything Bubák does

- Training, grooming (coat and nails), shows, agility, walks and personality traits now work for
  Bubák's friend too. The Club and the Walk screen have a Bubák / friend switch at the top; the
  choice is remembered, and closing the window always goes back to Bubák.
- Each rabbit has their own trick stars, focus, master's certificates, coat, nails, agility skill,
  personality, show waits, Tricks routine and daily walk. Coats dull, nails grow and focus refills
  for both, also while the game is closed. Saved under "mate_skills"; older saves start the
  friend from scratch.
- Shared by the household: league points, the Bunny Games, duels, the daily agility course,
  places and souvenirs, the vaccination (one vet visit does both) and ribbons. Each new ribbon
  records who won it (Trophies tab, journal pages). Goals count whichever rabbit does better.
- The stage, the judges' table, the walk cutscene and their texts use the rabbit who's performing.
  Texts that said "he" or "Bubák" for whoever performs now use the name or neutral wording (English
  and Czech, no gendered past tense in Czech).
- The friend's personality works at home too: a little star poses for the camera, a lap bunny
  melts into a puddle, an athlete gets restless and does parkour, an explorer inspects. The
  friend's Journal page shows trick stars, coat, ribbons, certificates, nail and coat warnings
  and the personality box. The vet checks both rabbits' nails and coats.
- Playtest: a new section runs every feature with the friend and checks Bubák's skills stay his
  own, mid-session saves, loading, and that the friend poses for the camera.

## 2026-10-29 02:10 CET — hide the controls; the friend dresses up

- A new eye button (bottom left) hides every on-screen control: the status cards, the button row
  and the Move banner. Droppings and things behind them can then be tapped or dragged. Tap it again
  (or press Back) to show them. Photo, treat and selfie modes bring the controls back by themselves.

- Playtest: new checks for both. Two flaky checks fixed (a postcard counted after the game's own
  check had already sent the next one; an earlier foster in the Hall of Fame).

- **Advent calendar** on the wall in December: a door a day, a Santa hat on the 24th, a snow globe

- **Bubák's book:** 13 chapters opened by big moments, written in Journal > Memories; publishing it

  friend). 25 coins each; all three put a sticker on the month's calendar (+60), every 7 stickers

- **Seasons:** spring petals to chase, summer heatwave days (he melts flat on the floor, fewer
  zoomies), an autumn leaf pile on the balcony to dive into, snowy winter days to sniff. Falling
  blossom/leaves outside, snow on the balcony floor. The first time each season he finds something:
  blossom clip, straw hat, knitted scarf, snowflake clip (later years: coins and rose petals).
- **The veggie patch** grows parsley too; picking sometimes turns up a golden seed (a day to grow a
  golden carrot, the best treat). The bigger veggie patch (Shop > Balcony, 520) adds two beds.
- **The dig box** turns things up: buttons, marbles, bottle caps, an old coin, a tiny ring, and
  treasure map pieces. Eight make a map of the living room with an X: tap the spot to dig up 1,500
  coins and (the first time) a treasure chest for the flat. Journal > Bubák.
- **Random events:** thunderstorms (the bunnies hide trembling; comforting one builds trust), the
  neighbours' child at the door (lovely at Trusting and up, scary before), and hide and seek (only
  his ears show; tap to find him). One storm and one child a day at most.
- **Postcards:** a few days and a month after a foster goes home, their family sends a postcard (a
  crayon drawing and a few lines), pinned to the fridge in the Journal.
- **Hall of Fame and big brother:** fosters can stay longer than Friendly; each extra level makes the
  rescue's thanks half as big again, and those leaving at Trusting/Cuddly get a silver/gold frame.
  From Bonded, Bubák helps fosters settle in 30% faster and sometimes washes their ears.
- **Personality:** Athletic, Lap bunny, Little star and Explorer grow from what you do together,
  three levels each, but only the two strongest past level 1. Each helps a little (hang time,

  exercise), and level 3 brings a behaviour: sofa parkour, melting in your hands, posing for the
  camera, sniffing out every corner.
- **Photo album** (Journal > Album): 63 slots on 7 pages, including every foster bunny; your best
  photo fills each. A full page pays 200, the whole album 2,500.
- **Vet:** a yearly vaccination (90 coins; first a month in, old saves get a week); two weeks
  overdue means no shows, walks or playdates. Very long nails mean fewer binkies, a matted coat
  fewer likes.
- **Agility:** double bars (a shorter zone), tunnels (don't jump) and weave poles (tap each pole);
  perfect take-offs give a burst of speed, faults cost a moment. A daily course with a clock (faults
  +4 s), 40 coins for the first run each day, and a see-through ghost of today's best. Shows mix
  in tunnels, then double bars, then weave poles by level.
- Fix: a nipping foster bunny still gained trust overall (the cuddle before the nip outweighed it);
  a nip now costs 1.0 trust.
- 10 new behaviours, 12 new goals, new art (accessories, finds, chest, crops, icons) and a thunder
  sound. Playtest: random visitors and seasonal finds are held still except in their own checks.

## 2026-10-25 23:23 CET — wear and tear, a clearer agility course, bug fixes

- **Wear and tear.** Toys and amenities wear out with use by any of the bunnies (cardboard castle
  ~30 games, ball 70, tunnel 60, treat puzzle 60, dig box 80, treat ball 80, hut 90, hurdles 120,
  castle 140, bed 120 naps, teepee 160, lookout deck 200). Worn items look faded, the toy chest
  shows "worn N%", a message warns at 75%, and at the end it's loved to pieces: removed, a message,
  a scrapbook memory the first time, and it can be bought again. Presents and rewards never wear
  out. Stored items keep their wear.
- **Agility course reworked.** The bars, ground and jump are drawn together so what you see is
  what's judged: a bar counts as cleared only if his feet are above it as it passes his middle.
  A gold take-off zone on the ground (from his real hang time and the course speed) shows when to
  jump; the bar inside it lights up with "JUMP!" above it, and a take-off mid-zone gives "Perfect!".
  Smoother trot, an arcing jump and a landing squash; smaller bunny so the zone is visible; more
  hang time (0.54-0.86 s); slower show course (250 + 35 per tier). Space/Enter also jumps.
- Fixes found in a bug hunt:
  - Loading a named save took trust for the days since it was saved (named saves don't age).
  - Saves from before this update would have lost trust for days missed before keeping the bond
    existed; they get a one-time pass.
  - A one-trick routine scored as if it were a full routine: short routines now count missing
    tricks as zero.
  - Agility could be won by spamming Jump: a jump at thin air now needs a 0.8 s breather.
  - The Club's League tab wasn't translated, and it ran off the window in Czech (now scrolls).
  - Playtest: the trophy-shelf tap could hit a fame pop-up; the soak tool has a `late` mode
    (`-- --weightsim=late:6`) for the late game.

## 2026-10-24 10:32 CEST

- **Remote work: repeating the same job pays less.** Each recent shift of a job cuts its pay
  by 20% (100% → 80% → 60% → 40%, never below 30%). The cut wears off by one shift every
  30 minutes, and each job is tracked on its own, so switching jobs or taking a break keeps
  the pay up. XP, stars and records are unchanged. Job cards show "Done a lot lately: pay N%",
  and the results screen explains the cut. Tunable via `JOB_REPEAT_CUT`, `JOB_REPEAT_FLOOR`
  and `JOB_REST` in `scripts/catalog.gd`; saved per job in `job_tired`.
